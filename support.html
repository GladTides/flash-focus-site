Classic and Daily Focus

Follow the active rule shown in the game and choose the response that matches it. Daily Focus follows a UTC calendar schedule, so its reset may occur at a different local time depending on your location.

A result is ranked only when it is eligible and accepted by the service. Offline play, connectivity interruptions, or an unsuccessful submission may leave a result unranked. Do not assume a score was submitted unless the app confirms it.

Guest account access

Your guest account is tied to account information stored by the app. It may not have email sign-in or an independent recovery method. If the device or app data is lost, the account may not be recoverable. Avoid uninstalling the app or clearing its data if you need to preserve access.

Reports and blocks

Use the in-app controls to report a player or block a player. Blocking affects the app’s visibility and interaction behavior; it does not remove that player from official leaderboard rankings. Reporting and blocking do not guarantee a particular moderation outcome or review time.

Public usernames are moderated. No review time or appeal procedure is specified on this page.

Export or delete your account

Open Account & Privacy in the app to request an export or begin account deletion. Follow the confirmation and status instructions. A network error, sign-out, or missing response does not establish that deletion is complete.

Read the account-deletion information for the current flow and its limitations.

Technical help

When contacting support, include the app version, what you expected, what happened, and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send passwords, access tokens, another player’s private information, or an export containing information you do not intend to share.

Urgent safety concerns

The in-app report control is for concerns about Flash Focus usernames and player conduct. This support page is not an emergency-response service.

Age and jurisdiction

Public participation intended for users aged 10 and above. Jurisdiction: Worldwide.
