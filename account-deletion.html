Account & Data Deletion

Use the in-app account-deletion flow to request removal of your Flash Focus account and account-owned gameplay and profile data.

Operator: Glad Tides · Jurisdiction: Worldwide

Public participation intended for users aged 10 and above. For support, email mubashshirahmed@icloud.com.

Request deletion in the app

  1. Open Flash Focus and go to Account & Privacy.
  2. Choose account deletion and review the in-app confirmation.
  3. Complete the confirmation to begin the request.
  4. Use the deletion-status control to check whether the request is pending or confirmed.

What the implemented flow does

Account-owned gameplay and profile data remain while the account is active and are removed through the implemented account-deletion flow. The flow also checks deletion status so the app can recover when the original deletion response is lost.

Do not treat an error as confirmation

A network failure, sign-out, or missing response does not prove that deletion completed. If the app reports that deletion is pending or could not be confirmed, return to the deletion-status control when you can connect and follow the in-app recovery instructions.

Device data is separate

Clearing app data or uninstalling the app is not the account-deletion request. It may remove the local information needed to continue using a guest account, and account recovery may not be available. Request and confirm server-side deletion in the app before removing its local data.

Export or get help

The app also provides an export of eligible account data. For app instructions, see Support. For help, email mubashshirahmed@icloud.com.
