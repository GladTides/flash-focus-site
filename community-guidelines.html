Community · Flash Focus

Community Guidelines

Flash Focus is a game for quick rounds and friendly competition. These draft guidelines describe the behavior expected around public player names and game features.

DRAFT – NOT FOR PUBLICATION

Public usernames are moderated. Users can report and block players. Final enforcement and appeals wording requires review before publication.

Choose a safe username

  • Use a nickname rather than your legal name.
  • Do not include phone numbers, email addresses, home or work addresses, or other private contact details.
  • Avoid names that target or demean people, impersonate another player or organization, or falsely suggest that you represent Flash Focus or Glad Tides.
  • Do not use usernames to threaten, harass, or expose another person’s private information.

Play fairly

Use the game as intended. Do not manipulate scores or rankings, automate play or submissions, exploit defects, or interfere with other players’ use of the service.

Use report and block controls

You can report a player from the in-app safety controls when you have a genuine concern, and block a player to limit unwanted visibility or interaction. Provide accurate context when reporting and do not use reports to retaliate or harass.

Blocking changes visibility and interaction behavior; it does not remove a player from official leaderboard rankings.

Moderation and appeals

Public usernames are moderated. The app includes player reporting and blocking. The review process, available enforcement actions, escalation and appeal route, response times, and operational ownership are review required before publication. This draft does not promise a specific moderation result or timeline.

Age eligibility

Review required before publication. No minimum age or age-related consent threshold is established by this draft.
