About the service

Flash Focus Mobile is a visual game with Classic play, a Daily Focus challenge, personal progress and achievements, and optional public leaderboard participation.

Accounts and results

The app uses a guest account associated with the device’s locally stored account information. It may not include an email sign-in or independent recovery. Game results can be eligible for verification and leaderboard participation when the feature and service accept them. A displayed unranked result is not a verified public ranking.

Player names and fair play

Public usernames are moderated. Users can report and block players. Follow the Community Guidelines, use the game as intended, and do not manipulate scores or rankings, automate play or submissions, exploit defects, or interfere with others’ use of the service.

Final enforcement and appeals wording requires review before publication. Blocking affects visibility and interaction behavior; it does not remove a player from official leaderboard rankings.

Privacy, export, and deletion

In-app Account & Privacy controls include eligible data export and an account-deletion flow. Account-owned gameplay and profile data remain while the account is active and are removed through the implemented account-deletion flow. Final retention wording requires review before publication.
