Who operates the app

Flash Focus is operated by Glad Tides. The legal identity and any public address of the operator have not been supplied for this draft.

Information used by Flash Focus

Depending on the features you use, the mobile app and its backend process:

  • Guest account identifiers that keep your account and game activity associated. A guest account is not an email-based sign-in or an account-recovery promise.
  • Player profile information, including a pseudonymous public player ID, generated player tag, and nickname.
  • Classic and Daily Focus game data, such as session status, score, streak, accuracy, reaction-time summaries, and verified-result information.
  • Leaderboard information for eligible results when public leaderboard participation is enabled.
  • Progress and achievement records used to show in-app milestones and progress.
  • Safety records associated with reports and blocks, used by the in-app safety controls.

Technical and operational records may also be processed to operate or troubleshoot the service. Their complete fields, purposes, and retention require review before publication.

Public and private information

A nickname and eligible result may be visible on public leaderboards when participation is enabled. Private account identifiers, export files, and moderation information are not leaderboard content. Use the available in-app controls to change public participation. Other people may have already seen, copied, or cached information previously displayed publicly.

Service provider

The mobile backend uses Supabase for applicable account and game services. The full provider list, processing locations, and any applicable international-transfer information require operator review. No hosting location is stated in this draft.

Retention and deletion

Account-owned gameplay and profile data remain while the account is active and are removed through the implemented account-deletion flow. Final retention wording requires review before publication. This statement does not set periods for backups, diagnostics, or other records.

The in-app export feature returns eligible account data. In-app deletion includes a status and recovery flow; clearing app storage on a device is not proof that the server-side account has been deleted. See Account & Data Deletion.
